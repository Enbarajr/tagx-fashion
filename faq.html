Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How to order on TagX Fashion? ▼
Browse our collections, choose your size and quantity, and click Add To Cart or Order via WhatsApp. Fill in your delivery details on the checkout page, and click Place Order. This opens WhatsApp with your pre-formatted order details ready to send.
❓ How to pay for my order? ▼
Once you send your WhatsApp order, our TagX team confirms stock availability and sends you our official UPI QR code / PhonePe / Google Pay / Paytm details. Pay using any UPI app and send a screenshot of the payment receipt!
❓ What is the delivery time? ▼
Orders are packed and dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation. Delivery usually takes 2 to 4 business days depending on your location across India. Live tracking links are shared directly on your WhatsApp.
❓ What is your return policy? ▼
We accept returns or exchanges within 7 days of delivery for unused items with original tags intact. Simply message our WhatsApp support team to arrange an exchange or return.
❓ What if I face a size issue? ▼
Don't worry! We offer free size replacements. If your shirt or jeans feel too tight or loose, message us on WhatsApp with your order ID, and we will swap it for your preferred size.
